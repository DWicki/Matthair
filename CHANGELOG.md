# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Pair Matter devices (Wi-Fi and Thread) with Home Assistant over the phone's Bluetooth, using the Matter Server's BLE proxy protocol
- QR, manual code, photo and NFC setup codes; traffic-light pre-flight check; live progress with retries
- Device list and device page, rename, change room, ecosystems (multi-admin) and sharing
- Sign in with the Home Assistant login or a long-lived token; self-signed certificates after fingerprint confirmation
- English and German
