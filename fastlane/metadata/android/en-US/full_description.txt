Matthair adds Matter devices to Home Assistant from a phone without Google Play Services (/e/OS, LineageOS, GrapheneOS and other de-Googled Android systems).

The phone only lends its Bluetooth to Home Assistant. The Matter Server does the actual pairing through its built-in Bluetooth proxy protocol, so device credentials, your Wi-Fi password and your Thread network never pass through the phone.

<b>Adding devices</b>
• Guided flow: scan, check, pair, name
• Scan the QR code offline, type the code, pick a photo or tap an NFC tag
• Traffic-light check with one-tap fixes before pairing
• Wi-Fi credentials from a Wi-Fi QR code
• Live progress, automatic retries and plain-language errors

<b>Managing devices</b>
• Device list with online state, network, firmware and model
• Rename devices and change their room
• See and remove ecosystems such as an old Google connection
• Share a device with Apple Home, Alexa and others via QR code

<b>Requirements</b>
• Android 12 or newer with Bluetooth LE
• Home Assistant 2026.6 or newer (HA OS or Supervised) with the Matter Server app 9 or newer, administrator account

Matthair only talks to your own Home Assistant. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Matthair is an independent project, not affiliated with the Connectivity Standards Alliance or the Open Home Foundation.
